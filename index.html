Privacy Policy

Steps · Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Steps app ("Steps", "we", "us", or "our") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Steps is a step-tracking app available on iOS and Android. By using Steps, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to make the app work:

  • Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and a display name. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth.
  • Step data. With your explicit permission, Steps reads your daily step counts and the associated dates from Apple Health (HealthKit) on iOS and Google Health Connect on Android. This access is read-only — Steps never writes data back to Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • App data. To compute your goals and leaderboards, we store your step goal, streaks, group memberships, and your device's time zone.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to provide the features of the app — tracking your steps, calculating your goals and streaks, and powering group leaderboards.

We do not use your data for advertising or marketing. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties.

How Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored securely on Supabase, the backend service that powers Steps. We rely on Supabase's security measures to keep your information protected.

Health Data

Step counts and dates read from Apple Health (HealthKit) and Google Health Connect are accessed only with your permission and used solely to provide the app's features. You can revoke this permission at any time through your device's Health or Health Connect settings, and Steps will stop reading that data.

Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your information for as long as you have an account with Steps. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing petersen.larsm@gmail.com. We will delete your data from Supabase upon your request.

Children's Privacy

Steps is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Steps after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at petersen.larsm@gmail.com.
